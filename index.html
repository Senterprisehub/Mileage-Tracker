Made for everyday driving

Less admin. More clarity.

Useful tools for tracking trips and understanding vehicle expenses, with control over how you record each drive.

📍

Automatic & manual trips

Use background location and motion-based detection, or enter trips manually. Automatic detection requires the relevant Android permissions.

⛽

Fuel & running costs

Estimate fuel use, fuel expense, and maintenance using vehicle efficiency and the rates you set. Online city fuel-price lookup is available for India.

🚙

Multiple vehicles

Keep profiles for your vehicles, set their details, and assign drives to the vehicle you used.

🧾

Useful trip details

Organize business, personal, medical, or charity drives with notes and expense details like tolls and parking.

📊

Reports & backups

Export mileage and expense information as PDF or CSV, share reports, and back up or restore app data.

🔐

Local-first records

Trip and vehicle records are stored on your device. Internet access is used for online fuel-price lookup; location access is needed for tracking.
