Less admin. More clarity.

Useful tools for tracking your driving, understanding vehicle expenses, and keeping trip details together.

📍

Automatic & manual trips

Use background location and motion-based detection, or enter trips manually when you prefer. Automatic tracking requires permissions and may depend on device settings.

⛽

Fuel & running costs

Estimate fuel use, fuel expense, and maintenance from vehicle efficiency, fuel type, and rates in your settings.

🚙

Multiple vehicles

Keep profiles for cars, motorcycles, scooters, and other vehicles, then assign drives to the right vehicle.

🧾

Useful trip details

Organize business, personal, medical, or charity drives with notes, tolls, parking, and odometer details.

📊

Reports & backups

Export mileage and expense information as PDF or CSV, share reports, and back up or restore your app data.

🔐

Local-first by design

Trip and vehicle records are stored on your device. Live fuel-price lookup uses the internet and may not be available for every location or fuel type.
