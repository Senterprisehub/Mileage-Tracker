1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stop forgetting to log your business trips or guessing how much your daily drives
actually cost. Mileage Tracker is an intelligent, precision-engineered automotive
telemetry and expense tracking application for Android. Whether you drive a car,
motorcycle, scooter, EV, or manage multiple vehicles, the app automatically
detects when you start driving, tracks every kilometre with pinpoint GPS and
3-axis motion sensor accuracy, calculates real-time fuel and maintenance costs
using today's live city fuel prices, and generates audit-ready PDF and CSV/Excel
reports in seconds.

3. COMPLETE FUNCTIONAL FEATURE BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. INTELLIGENT AUTO-DRIVE DETECTION & MOTION TELEMETRY
------------------------------------------------------
* Configurable Speed Trigger (1 km/h to 15 km/h):
  Set the exact speed threshold (default 10 km/h) at which automatic trip
  recording begins.
* 3-Axis Accelerometer & GPS Fusion:
  Combines high-accuracy GPS Doppler/displacement speed with 3-axis linear
  acceleration (RMS motion filtering) to eliminate indoor GPS drift and false
  starts while stationary.
* Smart Latched Idle Auto-Stop Countdown:
  When you stop at a destination or traffic signal, a live stationary countdown
  timer (configurable: 5, 8, 10, or 15 minutes) begins. Sub-threshold movements
  under 10 km/h are treated strictly as Idle so walking or minor GPS drift never
  interrupts your stop countdown.
* 5-Second Sustained Resume Verification:
  If you start driving again before the idle countdown finishes, sustaining
  10 km/h or higher for 5 seconds automatically cancels the stop timer and
  seamlessly continues your active trip.
* Pause Auto-Tracking with Auto-Resume Timer:
  Going on public transit or riding as a passenger? Pause auto-detection for a
  preset duration (30 mins, 1 hour, 2 hours, Until Tomorrow Morning, or Custom)
  and the app will automatically wake up and resume tracking the moment the
  timer expires.

B. LIVE ACTIVE DRIVE HUD & LOCK-SCREEN PRIORITY NOTIFICATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------
* Full-Screen Live Telemetry HUD:
  Displays real-time speed (km/h), distance travelled (km), elapsed trip time,
  live fuel consumed (L), live fuel cost, current street/locality address, and
  idle auto-stop countdown progress.
* High-Priority Lock-Screen & Status Bar Notification:
  Pinned foreground notification updates every second with live kilometres,
  real-time fuel expense, current speed, and a one-tap "Stop & Save Trip" button
  visible right on your lock screen.
* End-of-Drive Expense Capture:
  Add tolls, parking fees, ending odometer reading, and custom trip notes when
  completing a drive.

C. MULTI-VEHICLE GARAGE & DYNAMIC COST ENGINE
---------------------------------------------
* Unlimited Vehicle Profiles:
  Add Cars, SUVs, Motorcycles, Scooters, Trucks, EVs, and Hybrids with custom
  fuel efficiency (km/L or km/kWh) and per-km maintenance/wear rates.
* Instant Vehicle Switcher on Recorded Trips:
  Forgot to switch your active vehicle before a drive? Change the vehicle on any
  saved trip directly from the Dashboard card dropdown or inside Trip Details.
  The app immediately auto-saves and recalculates fuel volume (L), fuel cost,
  maintenance cost, and total personal expense using the newly selected
  vehicle's mileage preset.

D. LIVE FUEL PRICE DETECTION & GLOBAL COUNTRY NORMS

* Daily Revised City Fuel Rates:
  Fetches live city fuel prices (referenced from GoodReturns.in 06:00 AM IST

  Automatically detects your current city via GPS and updates your active

* Custom Currency & Tax Write-Off Rates:
  Supports customizable currency symbols (₹, $, £, €, AED, etc.), distance

E. SMART WORK-HOURS SCHEDULE & TRIP CATEGORIZATION
--------------------------------------------------
* Day-Wise 12-Hour Work Schedule (Mon–Sun):
  Configure your work hours for each day of the week using intuitive 12-hour

* Manual Trip Logging:
  Log past or missed drives manually with start/end locations, distance,
